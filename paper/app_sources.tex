\subsection*{Data cards}
\paragraph{Veltri 2018 original dataset.} Distributed as six FASTA files
(\texttt{AMP.tr/eval/te}, \texttt{DECOY.tr/eval/te}) in the authors'
public repository. Positives are experimentally validated AMPs collected
from the APD; negatives are cytoplasmic proteins selected by the authors
as non-secreted decoys. The authors identity-reduced the pool before
splitting, so near-duplicates inside a partition are limited by
construction, but the split itself is a single random partition: results
on it carry split-specific variance that a single test number cannot
express, one motivation for our bootstrap intervals
(Appendix~\ref{app:ci}). We use the files byte-identically (hashes above)
and impose no additional filtering.

\paragraph{Feb2020 production dataset.} Distributed as a single zip from
the authors' site containing the retrained production model's dataset:
2{,}021 AMP and 2{,}021 decoy records. Unlike the 2018 release, no split
files are published; the site's news page reports only 10-fold
cross-validation means. Our study 14 therefore reproduces the protocol
(stratified 10-fold CV on all 4{,}042 records) but not the exact folds,
and the comparison in Table~\ref{tab:feb2020-verdict} is descriptive.
The dataset also contains records longer than the 2018 maximum and
records with the unknown-residue symbol \texttt{X}; study 14 retains both,
encoding \texttt{X} as unknown (Appendix~\ref{app:encoding}), because
dropping them would silently change the benchmark, the mistake study 13
made and study 14 corrects.

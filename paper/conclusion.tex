\section{Conclusion}
The original UniProt scale-up has a severe source-review confound: most
positive sequences are unreviewed while all selected negatives are reviewed.
Its AUROC $0.921$ and external APD6 score $0.926$ are retained as measured
outputs but cannot support the original clean AMP-recognition claim. The
Veltri 2018 test-set comparison is near the original AMPScanner model but
below ACEP. A full-record Feb2020 production-dataset CV offers a separate
benchmark comparison, with published-fold and homology-overlap limitations
spelled out. The YdcA screen result is a testable biological hypothesis;
signal-peptide ablation shows a major model-dependence warning. The shipped
CLI, tests, scripts, data and candidate audit make these claims inspectable.

\subsection{Future work}
Four extensions follow directly. (1) Activity-annotated benchmarking against
DBAASP once programmatic access is workable, replacing binary labels with
potency regression. (2) Alignment-based clustering (MMseqs2) to replace the
10-mer criterion, with a cluster-level bootstrap for intervals. (3) Structure
awareness: predicted contact maps or ESM-style embeddings as GNN edges and
features, which is the honest test of whether graph models help once the
graph carries real information. (4) Ablations of the encoding and the
training budget on the full 40k train split with more epochs, now that the
pipeline cost model is understood.
